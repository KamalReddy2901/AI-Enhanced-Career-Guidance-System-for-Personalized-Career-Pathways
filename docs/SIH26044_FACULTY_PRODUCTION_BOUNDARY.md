# SIH26044 Faculty Production Boundary

This checkpoint promotes faculty–industry collaboration discovery and detail into the authenticated production runtime without claiming unimplemented lifecycle persistence.

## Implemented in this checkpoint

- RLS-bound reads over `collaboration_engagements`, partner organizations, participants, and objectives.
- Production faculty explorer and collaboration detail routes.
- Purposeful identity minimization: identities hidden by authorization remain undisclosed rather than inferred.
- Reuse of the rich faculty presentation components with demo-only local milestone actions explicitly disabled in production.

## Not yet implemented

- persisted faculty expression of interest;
- proposal/approval transitions;
- persisted milestones;
- persisted collaboration outcomes;
- production authoring of collaboration engagements.

Those capabilities remain target architecture and must not be represented as live until the authoritative workflow and audit contracts are implemented.
