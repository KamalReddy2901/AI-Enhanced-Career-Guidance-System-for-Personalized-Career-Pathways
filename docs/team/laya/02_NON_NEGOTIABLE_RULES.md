# Laya — Non-Negotiable Rules

1. Do not calculate, approximate or relabel readiness in UI code. Render the canonical versioned result.
2. Never show a “match percentage,” hiring probability, candidate rank or employability score.
3. Display `UNKNOWN` as insufficient current information/evidence, not failure or lack of skill.
4. Keep eligibility, requirement state, evidence coverage, preferred requirements, experience, logistics and learning distance distinguishable.
5. Never use RIASEC, values, aspirations, counselor notes, financial constraints or other Engine A/private fields in opportunity ordering or readiness.
6. Filters must reflect student-chosen criteria; they must not silently auto-reject or hide opportunities through readiness.
7. Preserve opportunity and readiness version IDs. Warn and fail safely when they do not align.
8. Show unresolved skill language literally; never guess a canonical mapping.
9. Evidence badges must retain provenance and scoped verification meaning.
10. Link to Manvil/Madhu workflows; do not rebuild their actions or copy mutable state.
11. Controlled/demo opportunities must be visibly labeled. Do not claim live NCS, AICTE, NATS/NAPS, SIDH or employer feeds.
12. Do not edit Engine B, shared domain/service contracts, frozen migrations, Worker authority or other owners' directories without written approval.

Stop immediately if a desired display field is absent from the canonical contract. Raise a minimal contract request rather than creating an untyped parallel model.
