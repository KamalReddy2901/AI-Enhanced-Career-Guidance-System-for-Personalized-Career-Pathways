# Nipun — Non-Negotiable Rules

1. Faculty are first-class participants in training, FDP, consultancy, research, mentoring and workshops—not merely student verifiers.
2. Do not force all opportunity types into one identical workflow; respect type-specific lifecycle and fields.
3. Institution analytics must show cohort/filter context, denominator, freshness and limitations.
4. Suppress or safely aggregate small cohorts; never expose identifiable student details through charts or drilldowns.
5. Never use private Engine A inputs or create individual candidate/faculty rankings.
6. Separate observed facts, inference and recommendation in dashboard copy.
7. Analytics do not automatically trigger intervention, funding, eligibility or performance decisions.
8. Every intervention has a human owner, explicit status and reviewable rationale/outcome.
9. Do not claim causal impact from correlation or controlled fixtures.
10. Do not invent SIS/ERP, policy, employer or government integrations.
11. Faculty verification actions route through Manvil's scoped workflow; do not duplicate it.
12. Do not change analytics/collaboration contracts, Engine B, migrations or other owners' components without approval.

Stop if safe aggregation, membership authority or a collaboration subtype is not supported. Label controlled behavior and raise the missing contract.
