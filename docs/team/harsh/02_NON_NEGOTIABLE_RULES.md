# Harsh — Non-Negotiable Rules

1. Opportunity requirements and versions are employer/human-confirmed. AI may suggest structure, never silently publish or decide.
2. Unresolved high-stakes skill language stays literal; never guess a canonical skill.
3. Do not create a candidate match score, hiring probability, rank, auto-shortlist or auto-rejection.
4. Recruiter ordering may use workflow facts (stage/date), not readiness as a proxy rank.
5. Applicant detail must come from the immutable, purpose-specific, consent-minimized snapshot/projection.
6. Never expose RIASEC, values, aspirations, counselor history, financial constraints, guardian data or unrelated evidence.
7. Consent withdrawal must block ongoing access; do not cache a bypassing full applicant object.
8. Every recruitment stage change is an attributable human event with history.
9. Recruiters may access only applications for authorized active organization/opportunity relationships.
10. Do not claim live ATS, employer, NCS, AICTE or other integrations without implemented credentials and verification.
11. Do not reconstruct or weaken the production projection allowlist.
12. Do not edit Engine B, migrations, trusted authority, student/verifier/faculty components or shared contracts without approval.

Stop if the desired authoring or stage action lacks a canonical field/authorized method. Raise a minimal shared contract request.
